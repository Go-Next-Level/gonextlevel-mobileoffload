# MobileOffload Content and Authority Roadmap

## Highest value: evidence and trust

1. Approve and publish the first verified deployment case study with source records, methodology, gross/net definitions, limitations, and written customer/partner permission.
2. Reconcile the privacy policy with actual LeadConnector and analytics behavior through legal review.
3. Document current carrier/network relationships, geography, technical requirements, and the exact MobileOffload role.
4. Replace any remaining homepage commercial terms with an owner-approved summary linked to current written terms.
5. Publish an evidence policy and revision history when claims change.

## Technical depth

- Model/firmware-specific guides for the network platforms actually supported and tested.
- A downloadable, non-sensitive network inventory worksheet.
- Authentication troubleshooting based on verified deployments.
- Security architecture diagrams reviewed by a qualified network engineer.
- Reporting examples with synthetic/redacted data clearly labeled.

## External authority

- Customer testimonials only with written permission.
- Independent MSP, enterprise-networking, and telecom partner pages that describe the real relationship.
- Relevant technical conference talks, webinars, or publications.
- Accurate LinkedIn/company profiles and industry directory entries with consistent business identity.
- Earned coverage based on original, verifiable deployment data—no fabricated backlinks or mass unsolicited outreach.

## Measurement

- Verify Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Submit the sitemap after production launch and monitor index coverage.
- Track core guide queries, assessment starts, successful submissions, and source-page assisted conversions.
- Run the benchmark prompt set monthly; record real citations without simulating results.
- Review provider comparison facts quarterly.

## Future pages only when evidence supports them

- Detailed carrier participation guide by program and geography.
- Provider-vs-provider comparisons where material differences and current sources exist.
- Contract and settlement glossary reviewed by counsel.
- Additional industries with genuine operational differentiation and demand.

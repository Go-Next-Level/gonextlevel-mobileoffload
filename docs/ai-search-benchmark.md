## Evaluation notes

- A scripted model response is not equivalent to a real search experience.
- Record whether the platform searched the web, the model/version if visible, geography, and login state.
- Grade citation quality separately from brand mention. A mention without an accurate primary-source citation may not be positive.
- Review inaccurate summaries and ambiguous entity relationships as defects to correct on-site or through verified third-party profiles.

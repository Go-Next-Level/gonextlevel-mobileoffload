## Cannibalization controls

- The pillar defines the concept; `/get-paid-for-wifi/` answers nontechnical buyer language.
- The companies page compares engagement models; it does not repeat the general definition at length.
- The earnings page owns rates, payout, and gross/net language.
- The Passpoint page owns authentication/security; the compatibility page owns equipment and network inventory.
- Industry pages own operational constraints unique to their venue type and do not use a keyword-swapped template body.
